# Step 3: Evaluate the Fine-Tuned Model

**Objective:** Use the Model Performance Comparison dashboard to compare your fine-tuned model against the base model and interpret the results.

## What to Look For

A well fine-tuned model should show improvement in:

- **Overall accuracy** — Higher percentage of correct classifications on the validation dataset
- **Category-wise performance** — Improvement across individual entity categories (equipment, events, installations, inventory, military groups, organizations, ships)
- **Error reduction** — Fewer misclassifications compared to the base model
- **Consistency** — Balanced performance across categories, not just strong in one area

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Navigate to the Fine-Tuning Lab

On the left side menu, click **"Fine-tuning Lab"**. The **Model Performance Comparison** dashboard loads, showing a full side-by-side evaluation of your fine-tuned model against the base model — including accuracy scores, a category bar chart, a performance radar, category details, error listings, and key insights.

![Model Performance Comparison dashboard full overview](/img/lab-03/step3_1.png)

### Step 2: Review the Top-Level Accuracy Scores and Performance Delta

Examine the two score cards at the top of the dashboard. The **Fine-tuned Model** card (green, marked "Best Performance") shows **92.31%** accuracy with 12 correct and 1 error. The **Base Model** card (purple) shows **84.62%** with 11 correct and 2 errors. The **Performance Delta** panel confirms the fine-tuned model performs **+7.69% better** than the base model.

![Accuracy score cards and performance delta showing +7.69% improvement](/img/lab-03/step3_2.png)

**What to check:**
- Which model is marked **Best Performance**?
- What is the fine-tuned model's overall accuracy vs the base model?
- What does +7.69% mean in practical terms for this validation dataset?

---

### Step 3: Read the Category-wise Accuracy Comparison Chart

Scroll down to the **Category-wise Accuracy Comparison** bar chart. Each category (equipment, events, installations, inventory, military groups, non-state-groups, organizations, ships) has two bars — blue for the fine-tuned model and purple for the base model. Note where the fine-tuned model's blue bar is taller than the base model's purple bar.

![Category-wise accuracy comparison bar chart](/img/lab-03/step3_3.png)

### Step 4: Hover Over a Category Bar to See the Tooltip

Click or hover over the **inventory** category bars in the chart. A tooltip appears showing: **Base Model: 50.00%** and **Fine-tuned Model: 100.00%**. This reveals the biggest single improvement — the fine-tuned model achieved perfect accuracy on inventory questions where the base model only got half correct.

![Inventory category tooltip showing Base Model 50% vs Fine-tuned Model 100%](/img/lab-03/step3_4.png)

### Step 5: Click the Inventory Bar to Open the Question Detail Panel

Click on the **inventory** bar to open the **Inventory Questions** side panel. It shows 2 questions from the uploaded CSV. For Q1 ("Show the inventory of Russian Aerospace Forces") — both models answered correctly with **inventory**. For Q2 ("How many platforms does Sweden have in service?") — the fine-tuned model correctly answered **inventory** while the base model incorrectly answered **equipment**.

![Inventory Questions panel showing Q1 and Q2 model answers side by side](/img/lab-03/step3_5.png)

**What to check:**
- Which category shows the largest gap between fine-tuned and base model accuracy?
- Which specific question did the base model get wrong that the fine-tuned model answered correctly?
- Which categories score 100% for both models? What does that tell you about fine-tuning's impact there?

---

### Step 6: Examine the Performance Radar Chart and Category Details

Scroll down past the bar chart to find the **Performance Radar** — a spider/radar chart that visually compares both models across all categories simultaneously. The fine-tuned model's blue shape is larger than the base model's purple shape, confirming broader coverage. Below the radar, the **Category Details** panels show a side-by-side breakdown of each category's accuracy with color-coded progress bars.

![Performance radar chart and category details side by side for both models](/img/lab-03/step3_6.png)

### Step 7: Review the Error Listings and Key Insights

Scroll to the bottom of the dashboard. The **Fine-tuned Model - Errors (1)** panel shows a single misclassification: "What are the different roles of equipment operated by the Japan" — Actual: **military-groups**, Predicted: **equipment**. The **Base Model - Errors (2)** panel shows two errors, including the same question plus "How many platforms does Sweden have in service?" The **Key Insights** section at the very bottom summarizes the evaluation findings automatically.

![Error listings for both models and Key Insights summary](/img/lab-03/step3_7.png)

**What to check:**
- How many fewer errors did fine-tuning produce?
- Which question does the fine-tuned model still get wrong?
- Do the auto-generated Key Insights match your own observations from the dashboard?

## Expected Result

The dashboard shows the fine-tuned model outperforming the base model with **92.31% vs 84.62%** overall accuracy (+7.69% delta), the largest single-category improvement in inventory (50% → 100%), and a reduction from 2 errors to 1.

## 📝 Note

In a real deployment, even small accuracy gains translate to significant time savings at scale. The remaining misclassification (Japan equipment roles → military-groups) points to an area where additional training examples could further improve the model.
