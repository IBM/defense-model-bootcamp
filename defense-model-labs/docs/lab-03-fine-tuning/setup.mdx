## 🚀 Getting Started with Fine Tuning

Welcome to the hands-on portion of the lab! In this section, you'll set up your environment and get ready to work with the ARGUS AI fine-tuning interface.

### What You'll Be Doing

This lab is designed to be hands-on and visual. You'll:

- Access the ARGUS AI Fine-tuning Lab environment
- Configure the Model Performance Comparison tool by selecting models and uploading an evaluation dataset
- Run the comparison and wait for it to process
- Analyze the results dashboard to understand the impact of fine-tuning

## 🖥️ Environment Setup Steps

Follow these steps to get your lab environment ready before proceeding to Step 1.

### Step 1: Access the TechZone Demo Environment

Click the **Launch Lab Environment** button above. You will be taken to the IBM TechZone demo page.

### Step 2: Log In to ARGUS AI

Once the environment loads, log in using the credentials provided by your instructor. You will land on the ARGUS AI home screen / dashboard.

### Step 3: Navigate to the Fine-Tuning Lab

From the ARGUS AI dashboard, navigate to the **Fine-tuning Lab** section in the left side menu. This is your starting point for Step 1.
