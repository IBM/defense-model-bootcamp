# Background

## What is an After Action Report (AAR)?

![After Action Review](/img/lab-01/After_Action_Review_during_CSTX_86-17-03.jpg)

An **After Action Report (AAR)** is a structured document used by military organizations to:

- Document what happened during an operation or training exercise
- Analyze what went well and what could be improved
- Capture lessons learned for future operations
- Record equipment performance and personnel actions
- Provide accountability and historical record

AARs are critical for continuous improvement in military operations. They contain valuable information about:
- Mission objectives and outcomes
- Equipment used and its performance
- Personnel involved and their roles
- Environmental conditions
- Tactical observations and recommendations
