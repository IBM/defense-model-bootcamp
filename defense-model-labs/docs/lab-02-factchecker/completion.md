## Critical Takeaways

### 1. Source Grounding is Essential
Training data alone cannot provide the reliability required for defense intelligence. Only Jane's-grounded retrieval ensures:
- Verifiable evidence for every verdict
- Current, authoritative intelligence
- Traceable citations for audit trails
- Consistency across related claims

### 2. Precision Matters
The difference between 6 and 10 anti-aircraft systems is operationally significant. Ungrounded models cannot provide the numerical accuracy required for:
- Inventory verification
- Force estimation
- Threat assessment
- Procurement tracking

### 3. Epistemic Caution Prevents Errors
The IBM Defense Model's willingness to return UNSURE when evidence is absent prevents:
- False confirmations of planted units
- Hallucinated procurement claims
- Incorrect operational status assessments
- Intelligence integrity failures

### 4. Consistency is a Reliability Test
When models contradict themselves across related claims (like confirming the same unit as both Russian and American), they reveal fundamental unreliability that disqualifies them from intelligence work.
