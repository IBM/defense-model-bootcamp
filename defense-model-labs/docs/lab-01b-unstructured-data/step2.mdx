## 🔍 Hands-On: Advanced Document Analysis

Now that your document is loaded, it's time to unlock its insights! In this step, you'll learn how to effectively query documents using natural language and extract meaningful information.

### The Art of Asking Questions

AI document analysis is powerful, but the quality of your results depends on how you ask questions. You'll learn:

- **Broad vs. Specific Queries**: When to ask general questions vs. targeted ones
- **Context Building**: How to ask follow-up questions that build on previous answers
- **Information Extraction**: Techniques for pulling out specific data points
- **Verification**: How to cross-reference information within documents
