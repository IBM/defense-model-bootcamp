# Query 2: Operations with Location, Branch, and Mission Outcome

**Objective:** Retrieve all operations alongside their location coordinates, military branch, and mission outcome to enable geographic and outcome-based analysis.

:::warning
Results may vary. ARGUS AI uses a large language model to generate SQL queries and interpret data. The exact values, row counts, and phrasing of responses may differ slightly from what is shown in this guide due to the non-deterministic nature of LLM outputs. Focus on the overall pattern rather than exact numbers.
:::

## Instructions

1. In the middle panel, click **"Sample Questions"**.
![Business Intelligence menu item](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step2_1.png)
2. In the chat input field, type or select **"Show all operations with their location coordinates, military branch, and mission outcome"** from the sample questions.
![Chat input field with sample question selected](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step2_2.png)
3. Wait for the response to load. Then, on the map, click **on any blue bubble** to display the location legend.
![Map view with operation location bubbles](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step2_3.png)
4. On the upper band, click **"Show"** under Reasoning to display the SQL query.
![Operations with location and outcome data](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step2_4.png)

## Expected Result

ARGUS AI returns a map and a tabular view listing all operations with their location coordinates, military branch, and mission outcome, sourced from the `Equipment_Performance_Metrics` table.

Key observations from the data:
- Each row surfaces an **operation name**, **latitude/longitude coordinates**, **military branch**, and **mission outcome** in a single flat view
- **Multiple branches** are represented across the dataset, enabling cross-branch comparison
- **Mission outcomes** vary across operations and can be filtered or sorted to isolate successful vs. unsuccessful missions
- The **Reasoning panel** shows the auto-generated SQL query selecting the relevant columns across all operations

You can toggle between the **Map** and **Table** views, or export the data using the **↓ CSV** button.

## 💡 Tip

The response may take up to 30 seconds — do not refresh the page. Results may vary slightly depending on the generated SQL query.
