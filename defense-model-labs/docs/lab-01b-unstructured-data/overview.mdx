# Lab 1B: Introduction to IBM Defense Model with ARGUS AI

**Tools:** IBM Defense Model, ARGUS AI

## Overview

### Lab Overview

- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Prerequisites:** None

### Lab Steps Overview

This lab consists of 5 hands-on steps:

1. **[Lab Environment Setup](./setup)** - Access the demo environment and navigate to the Business Intelligence section
2. **[Operational Tempo by Month](./step1)** - Analyze how many operations were conducted each month and visualize tempo trends over time
3. **[Operations with Location, Branch, and Mission Outcome](./step2)** - Retrieve all operations alongside their coordinates, military branch, and mission outcome
4. **[Equipment Issues Ranked](./step3)** - Aggregate and rank equipment issues across all operations to surface the most frequently occurring problems
5. **[Equipment Performance Rating](./step4)** - Identify which equipment systems have the highest performance ratings across all operations

### What You'll Learn

Through these steps, you'll experience:

- **AI-powered Business Intelligence** that surfaces operational patterns from structured defense data
- **Natural language querying** over live data without writing SQL
- **Geographic and outcome analysis** by visualizing operation locations and mission results
- **Equipment intelligence** through aggregated issue rankings and performance ratings
- **The IBM Defense Model** combining cutting-edge AI with domain expertise
