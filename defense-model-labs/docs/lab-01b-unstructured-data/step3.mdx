# Query 3: Equipment Issues Aggregated and Ranked Across Operations

**Objective:** Aggregate and rank equipment issues across all operations to identify which problems occur most frequently and where attention is most needed.

## Instructions

1. In the middle panel, click **"Sample Questions"**.
![Business Intelligence menu item](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step3_1.png)
2. In the chat input field, type or select **"Aggregate and rank equipment issues across operations"** from the sample questions.
![Chat input field with sample question selected](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step3_2.png)
3. Wait for the response to load. Then, on the upper band, click **"Show"** under Reasoning to display the SQL query.
![Response with Reasoning panel](/img/lab-01b/lab-01B_Step3_3.png)

## Expected Result

ARGUS AI returns a ranked list of equipment issues aggregated across all operations, sourced from the `Equipment_Performance_Metrics` table.

Key observations from the data:
- The **top-ranked issues** represent the most frequently occurring equipment problems across the full dataset
- **Issue frequency** varies significantly — a small number of issue types account for the majority of reported incidents
- The ranking enables **prioritization** of maintenance and procurement decisions based on operational impact
- The **Reasoning panel** shows the auto-generated SQL query grouping, counting, and ordering issues by occurrence across all operations

You can toggle between the **Chart** and **Table** views, or export the data using the **↓ CSV** button.

## 💡 Tip

The response may take up to 30 seconds — do not refresh the page. Results may vary slightly depending on the generated SQL query.
