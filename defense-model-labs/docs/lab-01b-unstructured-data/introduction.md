# Introduction

## Lab Process Flow

The diagram below shows the end-to-end pipeline this lab is built around:

![Lab Process Flow](/img/lab-01b/lab-flow.png)

### Discover → Enrich → Govern (Optional) → Access

IBM technologies act as the foundational trust layer, providing responsible governance, security, risk management, compliance, and policy enforcement throughout the data lifecycle.

---

### Discover

- Collect and catalog structured and unstructured data from enterprise sources.
- Capture metadata related to defense operations and business context to establish a comprehensive data inventory.

---

### Enrich

Use the Defense Model to extract, classify, and structure information from unstructured content — documents like AARs, doctrines, emails, contracts, reports, and more. Transform unstructured data into analyzable structured formats through:

- **Entity extraction** — identify personnel, equipment, locations, and organizations
- **Data classification and tagging** — apply controlled vocabularies and classification labels
- **Document intelligence and OCR** — process scanned documents and image-based content
- **Relationship mapping** — surface connections between entities across documents
- **Business glossary generation** — auto-generate definitions and semantic context

Enrich metadata with business definitions, lineage, and semantic relationships.

> **Outcome:** Raw content becomes governed, searchable, and analytics-ready structured data.

---

### Govern *(Optional)*

- Apply defense-related controls and governance policies on the enriched data.
- Automate compliance, policy enforcement, and data protection workflows.
- Generate governance artifacts and knowledge graphs that connect data assets, policies, business terms, and regulatory requirements.

---

### Monitor & Visualize

- Provide a unified view of metadata, policies, compliance status, and data lineage.
- Manage taxonomies and assess governance effectiveness across the organization.

---

### Access

- Enable natural language search and AI-powered assistants.
- Allow users to discover, understand, and interact with trusted enterprise data using conversational queries.
