# Step 5: Examine the Entity Table

**Objective:** Review the structured presentation of extracted entities.

## Instructions

1. Examine the entity table displayed by ARGUS AI
2. Note the organization of information:
   - Entity name or designation
   - Entity type/category
   - Context (where it appears in the document)
   - Frequency (how often it's mentioned)
3. Look for patterns in the data:
   - Which equipment types are most frequently mentioned?
   - Are there any unexpected entities?
   - Is the categorization accurate?

## What to Look For

- **Completeness:** Are all major entities captured?
- **Accuracy:** Are entities correctly categorized?
- **Context:** Does the system preserve important contextual information?

## 📊 Analysis Insight

The entity table transforms narrative text into queryable data, enabling analysts to quickly identify key assets, participants, and locations across multiple documents.
