# Phase 1: Discover — Review the Automated Scan Workflow

Get oriented: see how QSE connects to GCM through the Jenkins pipeline before diving into findings.

## Part 1: Review the QSE Automated Workflow

Before looking at specific vulnerabilities, it helps to understand how the pieces fit together. QSE scans your application code, Jenkins automates the build and scan process, and GCM collects the results in one place. This first step gives you a bird's-eye view of that workflow.

1. In your browser, click the bookmark labeled **"QSE Workflow"** (labeled **A** on your screen).

   This opens a diagram showing how GCM, QSE, and the Jenkins CI/CD pipeline work together.

   ![QSE Automated Workflow diagram showing GCM, QSE, and Jenkins pipeline](/img/lab-01/qse-workflow-diagram.jpg)

2. Take a moment to review the diagram. You will follow this exact flow during the lab.
