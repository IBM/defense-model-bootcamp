# Lab 1: Discover and Inventory CBOM

**Tools:** Quantum Safe Explorer (QSE) with IBM Bob

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

- Scan an application environment to identify all cryptographic assets in use
- Generate a **Cryptography Bill of Materials (CBOM)** for the scanned environment
- Interpret CBOM findings and identify assets at quantum risk
- Use **IBM Bob** to explore and query the resulting cryptographic inventory

## Estimated Duration

~45 minutes
