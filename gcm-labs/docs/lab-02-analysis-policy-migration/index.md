# Lab 2: Analysis, Policy & Migration

**Tools:** Guardium Cryptography Manager (GCM) + HashiCorp Vault

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

- Analyze the CBOM produced in Lab 1 to identify cryptographic risk exposure
- Define and enforce quantum-safe cryptographic policies in **IBM Guardium Cryptography Manager**
- Connect GCM to **HashiCorp Vault** for centralized secret management
- Initiate a migration plan for non-compliant cryptographic assets

## Estimated Duration

~60 minutes

## Prerequisites

Complete **[Lab 1: Discover and Inventory CBOM](../lab-01-discover-cbom-qse/index.md)** before starting this lab.
